Introduction

This short guide is an introduction to my signature "Already Her" ™ Method that helps women learn to do less to feel MORE loved!

In this guide, we will get clear on:

  • What's going on: what's showing up in your life
  • Identify the underlying pattern and where it came from: because we can't change what we don't know is happening
  • Beginning small changes: look at how beginning to make small changes, to start doing things differently, but NOT trying to change too much at once begins the process of you forming a new empowered version of you who gets more of what she wants while doing LESS!
Let's dive in!
Page 4

What's Happening

You might notice that you seem to be doing A LOT for other people:

  • Saying yes when you want to say no
  • Helping others even when you're tired
  • Putting others first most of the time

But you also may have noticed that you may not actually FEEL particularly loved. Instead, you might feel:

  • tired
  • unseen
  • a bit resentful
  • like no one really notices you
  • Scared to change in case no one likes you anymore!
Page 5

The Problem

The difficulty when you became an overgiver is you notice...

"I seem to be doing a lot but I don't really feel loved/appreciated!" — Which sucks!

And you can feel stuck in a catch 22 situation of feeling fed up giving a lot/getting a little... But nervous to give up doing/possibly risk losing what you have. And that's what can keep us stuck!

Because sadly, doing more for people does not always create more love! In fact, sometimes it creates:

  • dependence
  • imbalance
  • quiet resentment (in you)

Love that FEELS nurturing... requires other things that right now may feel scary and unfamiliar! It requires:

  • honesty
  • balance
  • Space
  • boundaries
  • you being yourself
Pages 6–7

Next Steps

When you've had this realisation, it's tempting to try and turn your whole life around overnight! But this is also something that can feel: Scary, daunting, dysregulating.

Because you built an identity on this! And identity does NOT like being challenged! It doesn't like change! It fears it... and it desperately wants... to stay safe! To stay the same!

And you may have experienced this before if you've ever: Tried speaking up but couldn't, Tried saying no but it felt too hard! Because you have an inner-child that never learned she had rights — never learned it was ok to speak up.

So you need to take change slow... WHILE regulating your nervous system! Because small incremental changes are HOW we build a new identity WITHOUT it feeling awful, scary, overwhelming and dysregulating!

Here are some small things you could consider trying:

  • Pause before saying yes
  • Ask: "Do I actually want to do this?"
  • Do one small thing for yourself each day
  • Let some messages wait before you reply
  • Say one honest sentence you normally hold back

This will feel strange at first! That is perfectly normal! You are learning a new pattern! And it will take time for new actions to feel normal.

💖 *Bonus: Don't forget to praise yourself EVERY time you do something out of your comfort zone! And tell your inner child you're proud of her... Being brave, Trying new things! Because we ALL thrive with encouragement!
