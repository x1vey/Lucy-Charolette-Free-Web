THE RECLAIMING ME JOURNAL

A 30 Day Healing Journey After Narcissistic Abuse

WELCOME & HOW TO USE THIS JOURNAL

If you've been through an experience that left you doubting your reality, questioning your worth, and wondering how you lost connection with who you truly are, this journal is for you.

Healing isn't about becoming someone new. It's about remembering who you were before the confusion, the self-doubt, and the emotional exhaustion.

1
Take your time. Complete one day at a time.
2
Write honestly and without judgement.
3
Be gentle with yourself throughout the process.

Part 1

UNDERSTANDING WHAT HAPPENED

Before you can fully reclaim yourself, you need to understand the story. Survivors often ask, "How did I end up here?" Use these foundational prompts to help you get to the bottom of what actually happened to you.

The Root Childhood Blueprint
What did I have to do as a child to receive love, approval, or validation? Was love given freely, or was it conditional on my behaviour, achievements, or keeping the peace?
Adult narcissistic abuse is rarely our first encounter with conditional love or walking on eggshells. Sometimes the blueprint was laid out in childhood.
Childhood Blueprint Internalised Beliefs
What silent messages did I internalise growing up?
(e.g., "My needs don't matter", "My boundaries are selfish", "I am only valuable for what I can do for others")
Slow Erosion Adult Relationships
What parts of myself, activities, or friendships became smaller or faded away during my adult relationship(s)?
Survival Mode Adaptation
Who did I have to become to survive? (The peacekeeper, the people pleaser, the over-explainer, the caretaker, the perfectionist?)
Acknowledge the truth. You weren't weak. You adapted.
