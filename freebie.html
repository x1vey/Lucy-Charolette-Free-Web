The Core Pattern

The Problem

The difficulty when you became an overgiver is you notice:

"I seem to be doing a lot, but I don't really feel loved or appreciated!" — Which sucks!

And you can feel stuck in a catch-22 situation of feeling fed up giving a lot / getting a little... But nervous to give up doing / possibly risk losing what you have. And that's what keeps us stuck!

Because sadly, doing more for people does not always create more love! In fact, sometimes it creates:

  • Dependence
  • Imbalance
  • Quiet resentment (in you)

Love that FEELS nurturing requires other things that right now may feel scary and unfamiliar! It requires:

❤️
Honesty
⚖️
Balance
🌌

Action Plan

Next Steps

When you've had this realisation, it's tempting to try and turn your whole life around overnight! But this is also something that can feel: Scary, Daunting, Dysregulating.

Because you built an identity on this! And identity does NOT like being challenged! It fears change... and it desperately wants to stay safe! To stay the same!

Because you may have an inner-child that never learned she had rights — never learned it was ok to speak up.

So you need to take change slow... WHILE regulating your nervous system! Because small incremental changes are HOW we build a new identity WITHOUT it feeling awful or overwhelming!

Here are some small things you could consider trying:

  • Pause before saying yes
  • Ask yourself: "Do I actually want to do this?"
  • Do one small thing for yourself each day
  • Let some messages wait before you reply
  • Say one honest sentence you normally hold back

This will feel strange at first! That is perfectly normal. You are learning a new pattern, and it takes time for new actions to feel normal.

💖 *Bonus: Don't forget to praise yourself EVERY time you do something out of your comfort zone! And tell your inner child you're proud of her for being brave and trying new things. Because we ALL thrive with encouragement!
